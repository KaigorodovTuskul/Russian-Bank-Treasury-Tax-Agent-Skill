# Example: OFZ clean price / NKD split (synthetic)

Assume one bond, clean purchase price 980 RUB, NKD paid 30, coupon cash 60, clean sale price 990, NKD received on sale 12; fees zero. Events have hypothetical dates.

- Cash reconciliation: `60 + (990 + 12) - (980 + 30) = 52 RUB`.
- Illustrative analytical split: coupon/NKD during ownership `60 - 30 + 12 = 42 RUB`, clean-price result `990 - 980 = 10 RUB`; total `52 RUB`.

**Not a legally certified tax result.** Confirm event dates, how past recognized coupon is excluded, issue terms, source edition, applicable special-rate predicates and tax bases before computing payable tax.
