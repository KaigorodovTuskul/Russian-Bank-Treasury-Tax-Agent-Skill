# Example: partial bond principal redemption (synthetic)

Acquired bond outstanding principal 1,000 RUB, purchase tax-cost candidate 960 RUB excluding NKD. Principal redemptions: 400 RUB and 600 RUB.

- Illustrative proportional tax-cost allocations: `960 × 400/1000 = 384 RUB`; `960 × 600/1000 = 576 RUB`.
- Remaining cost: `576` after first redemption, `0` after second.
- Illustrative residuals: 16 and 24 RUB.

**Research only.** Before applying to a real issue, verify Art. 280(6), issue amortization schedule, actual acquisition tax cost, fees and coupon separation.
