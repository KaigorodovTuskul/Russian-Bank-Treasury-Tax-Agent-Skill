# Example: subordinated bond write-down — mandatory stop

A bank owns an instrument with nominal 1,000 RUB and candidate tax cost 900 RUB. Issuer announces a 100% write-down. **Do not calculate an approved 900 RUB tax loss or 225 RUB "tax saving".**

Required first: official terms, legal write-down trigger, actual termination and settlement documents, date/effective law, own bank position and applicable tax base, previous income/loss deductions, relevant tax-expert approval.

Expected result: `BLOCKED`, with an issue/evidence list, not an automatic deduction.
