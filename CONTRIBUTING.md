# Contributing

Contributions are welcome: especially reproducible bond-case edge conditions, dated legal citations, contradictory court decisions and improvements to evidence checking.

Before proposing a tax rule: supply the exact controlling legal provision (with effective revision and any transitions), document its applicability to a **bank investor**, include contrary positions/decisions and add synthetic numerical examples with clearly identified assumptions. Do not submit full licensed legal database exports or bank-sensitive data.

Run the repository validation commands described in `README.md`. Any incomplete source validation must remain `PROVISIONAL`/`BLOCKED` instead of being hidden to make a test pass.

By submitting a contribution you agree that your original contribution can be distributed under the repository's MIT license; you must have the right to submit it.
