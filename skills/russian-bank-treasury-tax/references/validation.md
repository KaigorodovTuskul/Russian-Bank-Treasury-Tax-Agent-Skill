# Validation protocol (not yet completed)

## Level A — structural / arithmetic

Validate skill frontmatter, relative links, fixtures and arithmetic invariants. The scripts in the repository only do this level; they never claim legal verification.

## Level B — dated normative

For every case, check original statutory wording, cross-references, applicable effective period, transitions, market-status facts, required licenses and issue terms. Record article, subparagraph, edition and exact quote/location. A source URL without the pinpoint and date is insufficient.

## Level C — adversarial Legal Judge

Use an independent reviewer to argue the opposite tax position, seek adverse cases and later appeals, distinguish old factual patterns and old rates, and test withheld versus paid NKD, cancelled coupons, bankrupt issuer, negative indexation and partial write-down.

## Level D — expert Human Test

Bank tax lawyer / tax accounting owner signs off on scenario, evidence chain, rates, ledger reconciliation and control exceptions before the rules become operational. Store approvals without confidential tax data in the public repository.

**Current status: A only for small synthetic fixtures; B–D incomplete.** Tests passing in CI must not be advertised as production tax correctness.
