# Roadmap

## 0.1.0 — Research preview (this package)

- Portable Agent Skill and documentation in EN/RU.
- Conservative bond decision map, stop gates and evidence templates.
- Unit-tested synthetic fixtures; no legal production claims.

## 0.2.0 — Evidence and issuer-term validation

- Dated legislation source registry and pinpoint citation checks.
- Confirmed revisions and amendments for 2025–2026.
- Actual bond-terms documentation and dated quote histories.
- Expanded cases including negative OFZ-IN indexation, issuance discount, replacement and cross-border withholding.

## 0.3.0 — Independent Legal Judge + Human Test

- Review case history and adverse findings.
- Expert-approved controlled test corpus with traceable evidence.
- Explicit decision log for unresolved exceptions.

## Later

- REPO, interbank loans and deposits, equities, FX, derivatives, metals, tax-agent duties.
- Optional read-only MCP adapters for authorized legal repositories; avoid redistribution of licensed texts.
- More detailed bank ledger/tax cost reconciliation, privacy-preserving deployments.

No dates or delivery commitments are implied. Each domain must pass independent checks before being described as supported.
