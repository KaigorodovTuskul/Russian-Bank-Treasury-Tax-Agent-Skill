# Evidence maturity and scope

**Research snapshot:** 2026-10-14. This date documents the project starting point, **not** an assertion that all 2026 legal amendments were verified.

The initial bond research was performed for a Russian joint-stock commercial bank with a universal banking license, investing on its own account. The preceding research produced 15 provisional synthetic scenarios and multiple iterative review notes. The owner elected to defer the **final** legal judge review and expert human validation.

### What is implemented

- Portable Agent Skills instructions, source and version-control checklist, evidence statuses and high-risk stop rules.
- General tax-event inventory for standard and complex bonds.
- Small independently executable arithmetic examples, tagged `SYNTHETIC_ONLY`.
- Repository CI checking only structural and arithmetic integrity.

### What is not implemented or validated

- Binding dated opinion on current rates, case law or all specific exceptions.
- Production tax engine for any ISIN, currency or special-event structure.
- Validated universal bank professional-securities licensing facts.
- Legal validation of source claims in prior conversational drafts, final Legal Judge Test or Human Test.
- Tax rules for REPO, interbank borrowing/deposits, derivatives, equities or general FX dealing.

### Graduation criteria

A scenario may be marked `VERIFIED` in documentation only after controlling legal text, historical edition and cross-references, transaction and issuer terms, conflicting positions and calculation have been independently checked. Operational bank use additionally requires designated human approval and relevant internal controls.

No complete licensed КонсультантПлюс, ГАРАНТ or confidential bank documents are committed to this public repository.
